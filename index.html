ShiftSync

A calendar for nurses and shift workers

Enter your shifts once, as a weekly pattern or a rotation like "3 on, 2 off", and ShiftSync answers the questions that actually come up: is Jess working tonight, when are the four of us all off, who can cover Thursday.

  • Rotations, not repetition. Describe the pattern and the app fills in the months.
  • Colleagues side by side. Connect with an invite code and see their days next to yours, at the level of detail they chose.
  • Honest about what it knows. A day it has not synced is shown as unknown, never as free.
  • Cover requests. Ask for a shift to be covered and let colleagues offer.
  • Your calendar, your device. Export to the Calendar app, or subscribe from anywhere with a private feed link.

Free on the App Store. No ads, no analytics, no selling your data.
